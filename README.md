# Overworld Wild Encounters Mod for Pokémon Gen 1 Recomp

Replaces invisible random encounters with visible, real-time roaming overworld Pokémon sprites across Kanto, featuring **real-time Poké Ball throwing, overworld catching, stealth backstrikes, real-time combat, floating HUDs, and 100% full compatibility with the PokéPC Followers mod!**

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Visible Roaming Wild Pokémon**: See wild Pokémon moving around in grass, caves, and routes in real time.
- **Full Compatibility with PokéPC Followers**:
  - Seamlessly coexists with `PokePCFollowers`.
  - Automatically recognizes and commands your active follower Pokémon in real-time overworld battles!
- **Real-Time Poké Ball Throwing & Overworld Catching**:
  - Aim and throw Poké Balls directly at wild Pokémon on the overworld map.
  - Interactive parabolic arc trajectory and real-time 0–3 shake wobble capture sequence on map cells.
  - Consumes inventory Poké Balls (`Poké Ball`, `Great Ball`, `Ultra Ball`, `Master Ball`, `Safari Ball`).
  - Caught Pokémon are automatically added to your Party or sent to your active PC Box!
- **Stealth & Backstrike Mechanics**:
  - Sneaking up behind wild Pokémon without being spotted awards a **Backstrike bonus**, granting significantly boosted catch rates!
- **Overworld Combat & Follower Pokémon**:
  - Direct your active follower or lead party Pokémon to perform real-time attacks on wild Pokémon with particle slashes, floating damage numbers, and floating overworld HP bars.
  - Wild Pokémon display emotion bubbles (`!`) when alerted, charging aggressive players or fleeing if timid.
- **Dynamic Poké Ball Selector HUD**:
  - Displays selected Poké Ball type and remaining inventory count, complete with target reticles and catch notification banners.
- **Authentic Dispersal & Biomes**: Roaming species dynamically match location biomes for all 151 Gen 1 Pokémon with MORN / DAY / NITE time-of-day rotations.

## 🎮 Controls & Keybindings

| Action | Primary Key | Secondary Key |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Throw Poké Ball** | `C` | `R` |
| **Cycle Selected Poké Ball** | `Q` (Prev) / `E` (Next) | — |
| **Overworld Attack Move** | `V` | — |
| **Toggle Standalone Lead Mon** *(if no Follower mod active)* | `G` | `H` |

*(Note: Keybindings avoid `X`, `Z`, `Tab`, and `Backspace` to preserve standard Game Boy controls like B-Button, A-Button, and Select)*.

---

## 🛠️ Installation

1. Place the extracted `Gen1PC-OverworldEncounters-main` folder into your `mods/` directory:
   ```
   pokemon-gen1-recomp/mods/Gen1PC-OverworldEncounters-main/
   ```
2. Enable both **Overworld Encounters** and **PokéPC Followers** in the in-game Mod Manager menu!
